♞ Mangus — Support

Mangus turns your chess.com games into a coach — it analyzes your recent games with Stockfish, right on your phone.

Getting started

  1. Open Mangus and type your chess.com username.
  2. Tap Analyze my games. The app downloads your recent public games and analyzes every move on your device (this takes a minute or so the first time).
  3. Browse your games, tap into any one to step through it, and use Show best move to see the engine's suggestion at any position.

Common questions

Analysis is slow / stuck. The first run analyzes many positions on-device; give it a minute. Make sure you have an internet connection so it can download your games.

"No games found." Check the username spelling. Mangus reads standard (non-variant) public games from chess.com.

Do I need an account? No — Mangus has no login and never sees your chess.com password. It only reads public games.
